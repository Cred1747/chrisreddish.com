How the numbers are counted
  • The map, country filter and Papers tab use author-level locations (812 papers): a paper counts once for each country among its authors, and a line means authors in two countries co-wrote at least one paper.
  • The findings strip and region chart use paper-level affiliations (812 papers); a paper with authors in several regions is split evenly between them.
  • Same-country collaboration appears in the side panel and tooltips, not as lines. Circle sizes use one scale across all views. Countries in italics in the Papers tab are paper-level only.
